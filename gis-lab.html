    Where the data comes from

    • USGS Earthquake Catalog for earthquakes. Marker size shows magnitude.
    • Open-Meteo Air Quality API for air quality. These are modeled estimates, not readings from a single sensor.
    • USGS The National Map and OpenStreetMap for the base maps and rivers and lakes layer.

    Use the layers button in the map's top right corner to switch base maps or turn layers on and off.
